The Toolkit

Six Ways to See the Unseen

Each tool is built specifically for The Grand Trainer's entity model. Toggle what you need, keep the rest quiet.

👁️

Ghost ESP

Silhouettes every spectral entity through walls and geometry, with distance readouts so you always know what's drifting toward you.

🧱

Wallhack

Reveals the station's hidden corridors and sealed rooms. Map the Grand Trainer's layout without ever opening a door.

🗂️

Entity Dumper

Dumps every live entity to a readable list — IDs, classes, positions, and states. The fastest way to learn what the game actually spawns.

🚪

No-Clip

Fly through walls, floors, and ceilings to inspect out-of-bounds geometry and any ghost that thinks it's hiding.

🏷️

Named Hitboxes

Every spectral hitbox gets an on-screen label. No more guessing which shape
